# Launch your own

A Flex token pays the people who hold it, for as long as people trade it. You set the price range, the lock, and the tax. The pool is one-sided: buyers walk that range, and the liquidity stays locked for the time you choose.

- [Smarts for success](smarts-for-success.md)
- [Game guide](game-guide.md)

Open **Launch** in the Flex app. Connect a wallet. The rest of this section is what you are deciding, and what the chain will enforce.
