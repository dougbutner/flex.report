# Launch your own

You set a price range, a lock, and a transfer tax. Buyers walk a one-sided pool. Holders get paid from that tax for as long as the token moves. The liquidity stays locked until the time you sign.

- [Empower your community](empower-your-community.md): what holders get, and how the cost compares.
- [How your token works](how-your-token-works.md): the path, the fees, and the EASY hold.
- [Smarts for success](smarts-for-success.md): what to decide before you sign.
- [Game guide](game-guide.md): presale, rain, angels, jackpot.

Open **Launch** in the Flex app and connect a wallet. The full action list is [flexforex (`for3x`)](../smart-contracts/flexforex.md). `3asy` and `fl3x` use the same launch path and omit angels and the jackpot.
