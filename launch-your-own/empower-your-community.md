# Empower your community

Holders get a share of transfer tax every time the token moves. You do not run a rewards site. You pick the tax, the range, and the lock. The contract splits the transfer.

What that is worth:

- The pay can land as your token, or as another token you add.
- The pool starts as your supply. Buyers bring the quote. You cannot pull it until the lock ends.
- On `for3x`, an angel draw and a jackpot can take a share of reflection. That share is not an extra tax.

There is no setup invoice. Liftoff checks that you hold EASY on `mon3y`. It does not spend it. The amounts are in [How your token works](how-your-token-works.md).

## How this compares

Setup cost is a one-time charge, if any. Recurring fee is what keeps coming out of trades. Swap pool % is the AMM fee. The next column is how much of that swap fee comes back to you. Competition is tokens launched. A plus means the public count is a floor. A blank means the venue did not publish it.

Pump.fun is the largest by tokens launched. Bags and four.meme are the next two factories named in a 31 Aug 2026 comparison. That same week, by fees, the order was Pons, then pump.fun, then Flap.sh.
