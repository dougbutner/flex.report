## Generational gold

Generational wealth stored in gold. Grandchildren-approved, with inheritance functionality for any account. Pure liquid for Paxos Gold, GRAMS reflects GRAMS by default.

Holders can pass a % of rewards to any account (inheritance), with a custom memo (same tree pattern as WON, different action names).

Visit [flex.town](https://flex.town) to send reflections, change rewards, and more.
