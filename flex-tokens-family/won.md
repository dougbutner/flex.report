## Step into Paradise

We’re living in paradise on earth. There are thriving ecovillages all around the world.

WON is a collaboration with Temple Earth that tokenizes and links ecovillages one per month (max) so you can journey/live between them, and be take care of with tokens as fair exchange.

WON holders receive reflections in the current Ecovillage token, and are invited to come visit IRL.

As we start, WON is reflecting EASY tokens.

You can set any % to continuously buy one project’s token if you want to live there, or just swap to BTC, XPR, etc so you can earn some, support some.

WON is the first EASY-backed token.
